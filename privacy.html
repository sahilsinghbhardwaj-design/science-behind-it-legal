Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

"Science Behind It Uploader" (the "App") is a private, internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel "Science Behind It". It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

The App uses the YouTube Data API with one permission: youtube.upload. It is used only to upload the channel owner's own original videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and set their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.

What data is collected

The App does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people. It does not read viewers' data, comments, subscriptions, or analytics. The only stored item is the channel owner's own OAuth token, kept privately on the owner's devices and never shared.

Use of Google user data

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of the YouTube API is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
